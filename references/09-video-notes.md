## What the comments add

- "1, 2, 3, 5 arrangement" was the takeaway most viewers named: a small run of the sequence is
  enough for a layout.
- Several working designers said they reach the same results through hierarchy, focus and
  colour without the ratio; one warned that strict use makes boring work. Both are in
  `08-handover-and-balance.md`.
